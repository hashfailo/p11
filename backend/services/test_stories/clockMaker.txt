THE CLOCKMAKER

In the old quarter of Varanasi lived an elderly clockmaker named Dev.

Dev owned a tiny shop filled with hundreds of broken clocks. Every clock showed a different time, but Dev insisted that none of them were broken.

His granddaughter Tara often helped him in the shop. One evening, she discovered a strange silver pocket watch hidden beneath the floorboards.

The watch had no numbers on its face. Instead, it had a single word engraved on the back:

"RETURN."

When Tara opened it, every clock in the shop stopped.

Dev immediately told her to close the watch.

He explained that the pocket watch did not measure time. It measured moments that had not happened yet.

Tara laughed, believing her grandfather was joking.

Then the watch began ticking.

A minute later, someone knocked on the shop door.

Dev looked terrified.

"Nobody should be able to find us," he whispered.

Tara opened the door.

Standing outside was a young man covered in dust.

He looked exactly like Dev.

But Dev was standing beside her.

The young man looked at Tara and said:

"You have to give me the watch before midnight. Otherwise tomorrow will happen twice."
