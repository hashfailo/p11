THE LAST SIGNAL

Arjun Mehta was a radio engineer working alone at a remote communications station outside Hyderabad.

For three nights, the station had received the same strange signal at exactly 2:17 AM. It contained only three words:

"DO NOT RETURN."

Arjun assumed it was interference until the fourth night, when the signal suddenly included his own name.

He called his friend Maya, a journalist in Hyderabad, and told her what was happening. Maya asked him to leave the station immediately.

Before Arjun could answer, the lights went out.

A few seconds later, someone knocked on the locked door.

Arjun checked the security camera. There was nobody outside.

Then his radio turned on by itself.

This time, the voice said:

"Arjun, Maya is already here."

Arjun froze.

Maya had told him she was still in Hyderabad.

He grabbed the station's emergency drive and ran into the darkness.

At sunrise, Maya arrived at the station and found the building completely empty. On Arjun's desk was a single handwritten note:

"THE SIGNAL WAS NOT A WARNING. IT WAS A MESSAGE FROM TOMORROW."
